AThe files the app reads and writes

GermplasmPro runs in the browser and sends nothing to any server: every file comes in from and goes out to a folder on your computer. This appendix says which they are, what format the passport data require and which columns each downloaded table carries.

A.1 · What the app reads

FormatWhere and what for
.csv, .tsv, .txtBlock 2, Open a file (CSV, TSV or project): the passport, one row per accession. Block 7, Load my characterization (CSV): the characterization, which is joined to the passport by accession number.
pastedBlocks 2 and 7, Paste from a spreadsheet: a block of cells copied with its header row. In Block 2 the same window carries the guided template for writing the table from scratch (Section 2.4).
project .jsonBlock 2, the same open button: the collection, the characterization and the settings of Blocks 3, 8 and 9 saved earlier with Save the project (.json).
on screenBlock 8: the viability constants of other species (Add constants for a species) and the records of tests, regenerations, subcultures, checks and visits, which are typed into the app itself and go straight into the passport.

When reading a text, the app picks the separator on its own —tab, semicolon, comma or vertical bar— by testing which one splits every line into the same number of fields, not which one appears most often. It respects quotes, commas and line breaks inside a quoted cell, and the byte order mark at the beginning; if replacement characters appear when decoding as UTF-8, it reads the file again as Windows-1252, which is what a spreadsheet on Windows usually saves. Completely empty rows are discarded, a repeated header is numbered so as not to lose the column, and a column without a header is named as in the spreadsheet: Column A, Column B… (Columna A… with the interface in Spanish).

!A spreadsheet workbook is not read, and that is on purpose

An .xlsx, .xls or .ods file does not open; save it as .csv or copy and paste the cells. Reading it would mean bringing in someone else’s library of hundreds of thousands of lines for a problem that is solved in two clicks. The choice was for the app not to load a single line that is not its own (Appendix F): that is what lets it open with a double click, without installing anything, and work without internet.

A.2 · The passport formats

Chapter 2 gives the full list of the 49 descriptors —the 42 of MCPD v2.1 and the seven of the GP_ extension— with their name in the app and an example (Tables 2.2 and 2.3). Here is what the validation requires of each class of descriptor. An error is a value that cannot be; a warning, one worth checking.

Class and descriptorsRequired format
Dates · COLLDATE, ACQDATE, GP_GERMDATE, GP_REGENDATEYYYYMMDD, with hyphens where unknown: 201907--, 2019----. Error if it does not follow that pattern, if the year is before 1700 or after the current one, if the month is not from 01 to 12, if the day is not from 01 to 31 or if that day does not exist in that month (30 February).
Decimal coordinates · DECLATITUDE, DECLONGITUDESigned decimal degrees: negative latitude south of the equator and negative longitude west of Greenwich. A decimal comma is accepted and becomes a point on import. Error if it is not a number, if it falls outside −90 to 90 or −180 to 180, or if only one of the two is given; warning if it is exactly zero.
Degrees, minutes and seconds · LATITUDE, LONGITUDEDDMMSSH for latitude (173832N) and DDDMMSSH for longitude (0971952W); unknown minutes or seconds, with hyphens. If only these are given, the app computes the decimal ones on import; if both are given and differ by more than 0.02°, warning.
Numbers · ELEVATION, COORDUNCERT, GP_STOCK, GP_GERMPCTA number, with a decimal point or comma. Error if germination falls outside 0 to 100 or if uncertainty or stock is negative; warning if elevation falls outside −430 to 6,500 m.
Country · ORIGCTYThree-letter ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code (MEX, GTM, PER), converted to capitals on import. Error if it is not three letters; warning if it is well formed but not in the list the app carries.
Institutes · INSTCODE, COLLCODE, BREDCODE, DONORCODE, DUPLSITEWIEWS code: three country letters and three digits (MEX006). DUPLSITE accepts several, separated by semicolons. A malformed code is only a warning.
Closed lists · SAMPSTAT, COLLSRC, MLSSTAT, GEOREFMETH, GP_CONSOne of the codes in the table below, regardless of case. Any other value is an error.
Multi-valued list · STORAGEOne or more codes from the list, separated by semicolons (13;40). Error if any of them does not exist.
Free text · the restNo format. ACCENAME and OTHERNUMB separate several values with semicolons.

Empty mandatory descriptors —INSTCODE, ACCENUMB and GENUS— are errors, and the twelve highly recommended ones, when empty, warnings. An in-situ or on-farm record is not asked for STORAGE, ACQDATE, DUPLSITE or INSTCODE. The rules that cross two fields —status against collecting source, order of the dates, in-situ route against storage, safety duplicate in the same institution— are in Table 2.6 of Section 2.6.

BThe decision rules, gathered together

The scales and thresholds the app places next to each number, in a single list; when they are adjustable, the default value is given. They are guides for reading, not laws: what you know about your species, your genebank and your sites always prevails. What is worth doing in each case is in the decision rule box that closes each chapter.

CGlossary

TermWhat it is
Accession (accesión)A distinct sample of a species that the genebank conserves and manages as a unit, with its unique number (ACCENUMB), which is never repeated or reused.
Accidental duplicate (duplicado accidental)The same collection entered twice into the catalog, almost always with the name written differently. It protects nothing: it inflates the collection and disorders the analyses. Block 3 looks for it.
Blocking (bloqueo)Comparing only the pairs of accessions that share some key —the collecting or donor number, the start of the name, the genus with the cell, the taxon with the year and month of collection— instead of all against all. It is what makes searching for duplicates in a large collection feasible.
Code 128A linear barcode that accepts letters, digits and signs; the one for envelopes, jars and shipments. Any handheld reader reads it and types the number wherever the cursor is.
Collecting gap (vacío de colecta)A cell of the territory without any accession. In GermplasmPro the analysis is geographic and based on the collection, not ecogeographic: it uses no climate or soil layers.
Complementarity (complementariedad)Choosing sites one by one, each time the one that adds the most types not yet held. It gives the minimum set of cells that gathers the diversity of the collection.
Conservation route (ruta de conservación)The way an accession is managed today: seeds, field, in vitro, cryopreservation, DNA, genetic reserve or on farm. It decides the alerts of Block 8.
Core collection (colección núcleo)A small subset that keeps, with a minimum of repetition, most of the diversity of the whole collection (Frankel and Brown, 1984); it is characterized and evaluated in depth instead of the whole collection.
Core allocation (C, P, L, S) (reparto del núcleo)How many places in the core each stratum gets (Brown, 1989): constant, which favors small strata; proportional, which reproduces the imbalance of the collection; logarithmic or square root, which lie in between.
Cryopreservation (criopreservación)Shoot tips, embryos or pollen at −196 °C, in liquid nitrogen. The long-term backup of clonal material; it is checked by thawing a cryovial.
Darwin CoreThe standard set of terms for publishing biodiversity records, the one required by GBIF and other portals (Wieczorek et al., 2012).
Ex situ / in situAway from its place —in a seed bank, a field genebank, in vitro or cryopreserved— / in its place, where the population keeps evolving: the genetic reserve and the farm.
Exclusive type (tipo exclusivo)A type (species, crop, taxon…) found in only one geographic unit of the collection. If that unit is lost, the type is lost with it.
Field genebank or living collection (banco de campo o colección viva)Living plants in a plot, an orchard or an arboretum. The route for trees, recalcitrant species and vegetatively propagated crops; expensive and exposed.
Genetic reserve (reserva genética)A protected site where a wild population is conserved in situ, almost always of crop wild relatives. GP_CONS = insitu; its key goes in GP_SITEID.
Good-Turing coverage (cobertura de Good-Turing)1 − f1/N: the probability that the next accession you collect is of a type you already have.
Gower distance (distancia de Gower)A distance between accessions for mixed characters —numeric, categorical and ordinal— with empty cells: each character contributes its relative difference and the average is taken only over what both have measured (Gower, 1971).
GP_ extension (extensión GP_)The seven fields GermplasmPro adds to MCPD for management and in-situ conservation: active route, in-situ site and keeper, stock, last germination with its date and last regeneration. They are never confused with the standard and are exported separately.
Hill numbers (números de Hill)Diversity expressed as “effective types”: q0 is richness, q1 the exponential of Shannon and q2 the inverse of Simpson (Jost, 2006). Always q0 ≥ q1 ≥ q2.
Jaro-Winkler, LevenshteinTwo measures of similarity between texts. Jaro-Winkler (Winkler, 1990) compares accession names and rewards a common beginning; Levenshtein counts the letters that must be changed, and flags misspelt genera.
M method (método M)Building the core by pursuing, one by one, the classes of the qualitative and ordinal characters it does not yet have (Schoen and Brown, 1993).
Mantel test (prueba de Mantel)Correlation between two distance matrices —here, composition and geography— with its p-value by permutation (Mantel, 1967).
MCPD v2.1The FAO/Bioversity multi-crop passport descriptors, version 2.1 (Alercia, Diulgheroff and Mackay, 2015): 42 descriptors with a fixed key, the common way of writing the passport.
MD%, VD%, CR%, VR%The criteria of Hu et al. (2000) for judging a core: means that differ, variances that differ, range retained and coefficient of variation ratio (the relative variation retained) compared with the collection.
Multilateral System (Sistema Multilateral)That of the International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture; its material is supplied with the Standard Material Transfer Agreement (SMTA; in Spanish, ANTM). It is recorded in MLSSTAT.
On-farm conservation (conservación en la parcela)In-situ conservation on the land of whoever sows it, where the variety remains under farmer selection. GP_CONS = onfarm; the keeping person or community goes in GP_KEEPER.
Outcrossing / selfing (alógama / autógama)A species that crosses with other plants / that self-fertilizes. In a regeneration, each outcrossing plant contributes two independent gametes, and a selfing one, in practice, only one; that is why a selfing species needs almost twice as many plants.
Passport (pasaporte)What is known about an accession: what it is, where it came from, who collected it and when, what material it is and how it is conserved. The input of every block.
Principal coordinates (PCoA) (coordenadas principales)A two-axis map whose distances resemble as closely as possible those of a distance matrix, here the Gower one (Gower, 1966).
Priority index (índice de prioridad)The 0 to 100 score of an empty cell in Block 6: the sum of remoteness, neighborhood richness, state deficit and nearby exclusive types, each scaled from 0 to 1 by its weight.
QR code (código QR)A two-dimensional code with Reed–Solomon error correction. At level H it can be recovered even with 30% of the symbol covered, which allows the chayote to be drawn in the middle.
Rarefaction (rarefacción)The richness a unit would have with a fixed number of accessions; it compares units with different collecting effort (Hurlbert, 1971; Heck, van Belle and Simberloff, 1975).
Regeneration (regeneración)Sowing an accession to obtain new seed when germination or stock falls below the minimum. Each one is a bottleneck: regenerate seldom and with many plants.
Richness estimators (estimadores de riqueza)Chao1, Chao2, jackknife 1 and jackknife 2: how many types the collection would have if collecting continued, computed from the rare types (with one or two accessions, or in one or two units).
Safety duplicate (duplicado de seguridad)A sample of the same accession kept in another institution, so that a disaster does not take away the only copy. It is recorded in DUPLSITE; one in the same institution does not count.
Seed storage behavior (comportamiento de la semilla)Orthodox: tolerates drying to 3–7% moisture and freezing. Intermediate: tolerates drying, but not deep cold. Recalcitrant: tolerates neither drying nor freezing, and cannot be kept in a seed bank.
Silhouette (silueta)For each accession, how close it is to its own group compared with the neighboring group, from −1 to 1 (Rousseeuw, 1987). Its mean says whether the groups are real.
Slow-growth in vitro culture (cultivo in vitro de crecimiento lento)Plantlets or shoot tips in jars, with growth slowed by cold or by the medium, subcultured every so many months.
UPGMA, Ward.D2Two ways of building a clustering tree: UPGMA joins the groups with the smallest average distance (Sokal and Michener, 1958); Ward.D2, those that least increase the within-group sum of squares (Murtagh and Legendre, 2014).
Viability constants (constantes de viabilidad)KE, CW, CH and CQ: those of the Ellis and Roberts equation for a species. The app carries the published ones for maize; those for other species are taken from Kew’s Seed Information Database or from the literature.
Viability equation (ecuación de viabilidad)That of Ellis and Roberts (1980): with the temperature and the seed moisture content it gives the deviation σ of the life span, and with it how much the germination of a lot drops over the years.
WIEWS code (código WIEWS)The code of an institution in FAO’s world information system: three country letters and three digits (MEX006). It is used by INSTCODE, DUPLSITE and the other institute descriptors.

DWhen something goes wrong

Each chapter closes with its own table of problems for the block. These are the general ones, those that affect the whole app.

What happensWhat to do
A block says Load your accessions in Block 2 first.There is no passport in memory. Load your collection, open your .json project or use the example collection (Chapter 2).
When you open the app, yesterday’s collection is not thereIt is saved in the browser of that computer: another browser, another system account, a private window or having cleared the browsing data leave it behind. Open the .json project you saved.
The app does not open with a double click, or opens blankYour institution blocks local pages. Use Open GermplasmPro.bat (or server.ps1), which opens the app at http://localhost:9600, or the online version.
The file is an .xlsx and does not openSave it as .csv or copy the cells and use Paste from a spreadsheet (Appendix A.1).
Accents come out as “Ã©” or “�”The file is neither UTF-8 nor Windows-1252. Save it from your spreadsheet as “CSV UTF-8” and open it again.
The paste window says Everything landed in a single column.The separator is not the right one: pick it in the paste window. If you really copied a single column, you can go on.
The first data row was taken as headers, or the other way roundFix it with the The first row holds the headers box of the paste window; if there is only one row and it is a header, the app warns that there is no data left to import.
The coordinates carry a decimal comma (19,149)With tab or semicolon as separator, the app understands them, warns when pasting how many values use it and converts them to a point on import. In a comma-separated file, a decimal comma not enclosed in quotes splits the number into two columns: save it with semicolons or copy and paste from the spreadsheet.
Opening a .json says That file is not a GermplasmPro project.It was not written by Save the project (.json). If it is a table, save it as .csv.
I opened a project on another computer and my constants, my genebank assumptions or the pairs I dismissed are missingThe project was saved with an earlier version of the app, which only carried the passport and the characterization. Next to the Block 2 counter it says Could not save in this browser…The browser storage is full or the window is private: what you do only lives in that tab. Save the .json project now and before closing.
I downloaded several tables in a row and only some came downThe browser throttles consecutive downloads: allow them for this site, or use Download everything (ZIP) in Block 10.
The report does not open for printingThe browser blocked the window, and the app says so: download the report with Download the report (HTML) and open it with a double click.
The printed labels do not measure what their format saysThe scale in the print dialog is not 100% or the printer shrinks the sheet. Set it to 100% or “actual size” (Section 9.6).
The numbers do not match those of the manualThe manual uses the uncorrected example collection and the default values; a setting saved earlier (threshold, weights, assumptions) changes the result. Go back to the defaults or check the state the chapter starts from.

FThe tool: data, privacy, license and citation

F.1 · What the app contains

GermplasmPro is written in HTML, CSS and JavaScript without third-party libraries: not for reading files, not for the charts and maps, not for writing the .zip, the QR code or the Code 128, which are generated in the browser itself according to their standards. It carries no fonts and requests none from the internet: it uses the system fonts. The only outside items are two, declared in LICENSES-TERCEROS.md:

  • The Lucide icons (version 1.49.0) of the top bar, the figure studio and the Animations button, drawn with a thinner stroke, under the ISC license; those Lucide took from the Feather project also carry the MIT license. Both are permissive and compatible with GPL-3.0.
  • The Natural Earth outlines of the countries (1:110 million scale) and of the 32 Mexican states (1:10 million), simplified for the app, with which the maps are drawn and each accession is assigned to its country and state. They are in the public domain: they require neither license nor attribution, and are declared only so that it is known where they come from.

The formulas are those of the original papers of each method (Appendix E), and the code lists, those of MCPD v2.1. The viability constants included are those published by Ellis and Roberts for Zea mays; for the other species you supply them, with their source.

F.2 · The example data

The example collection of Block 2 is fictitious. Its collecting sites are real localities with approximate coordinates and elevations, to which the program adds a small offset with a fixed seed; the accession numbers, the names of people, the stocks and the dates are made up. It holds 46 accessions of 24 taxa and 18 genera, from seven countries, across the five routes (Section I.7). The example characterization of Block 7 is synthetic: the program generates it with a fixed seed for the loaded accessions —six quantitative characters drawn around a profile per genus, four qualitative and two ordinal, with about 8% empty cells—, so its groups reflect those profiles and not measured plants. F.3 · Privacy: what is stored and where

Everything is computed in your browser. The app makes no request to the internet: it does not send the passport, it does not query catalogs or portals and it loads nothing from outside; the maps use the outlines it carries inside. Only two links in the top bar go out to the network, and only if you click them: the one to the suite portal and Report a problem, which opens GitHub in another tab.

What the app remembers stays in the local storage of that browser, on that computer, under keys beginning with germplasmpro:: the collection and the time of the last save; the characterization; the genebank assumptions and the custom constants of Block 8; the duplicate threshold and the dismissed pairs of Block 3; the fields, header and code of the labels; the title, genebank and signatory of the report; the language and the theme. The top bar separately stores whether the sidebar is collapsed and whether animations are on. None of that leaves the computer, and it is erased when you clear the browsing data for that site; Empty, in the Block 2 table, erases the collection after asking for confirmation.

iThe backup is yours

Browser storage is a convenience, not a backup, and sometimes it cannot be used: with the space full or in a private window, Block 2 warns that it could not save. The .json project carries the passport, the characterization, the genebank assumptions, the custom constants, the dismissed pairs and the threshold, and the label preferences; the report title, the language and the theme stay in the browser. Save the project at the end of each session, export the passport every season and write down the constants of your species with their source.
